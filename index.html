BEORNE

ASHES OF PENITENCE

The war is over. The king is dead by his hand, his brother Cryn's face the last thing he saw before the Pit took him. Now Beorne wakes in a cell of black iron, with fire for a sky — and guilt for a jailer.

a CRYN: The Dark Reflection tale — isometric hell-prison RPG

HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP: Arrow keys / WASD to move. E or SPACE to interact. MOBILE: D-pad (left) to move, A to interact / confirm, B for menu. Walk into wanderers to fight. ATONE in battle to build CONTRITION — at full, unleash ABSOLUTION. Fire burns: the Cinder Charm halves flame damage. Your choices matter. Especially the chained one.
